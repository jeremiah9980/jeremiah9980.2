1 · Email and domain security

Fix domain trust before marketing

DNS is managed through Namecheap default nameservers. Google Workspace is the email provider.

1

Verify Google Workspace MX

The scan shows smtp.google.com, which is Google Workspace’s current single-record MX value. Confirm it in Namecheap.

Namecheap MX record 
Type: MX
Host: @
Value: smtp.google.com
Priority: 1
TTL: Automatic
2

Add SPF

SPF authorizes Google Workspace to send email for teleotitle.com. Only keep one SPF TXT record at the root.

SPF TXT record 
Type: TXT
Host: @
Value: v=spf1 include:_spf.google.com ~all
TTL: Automatic
3

Generate DKIM from Google Admin

Do not guess DKIM. Generate the exact host/value inside Google Admin, then publish it in Namecheap.

Google Admin path 
Google Admin Console
→ Apps
→ Google Workspace
→ Gmail
→ Authenticate email
→ Select domain
→ Generate New Record
→ Copy TXT host and TXT value into DNS
→ Start Authentication
DKIM DNS format 
Type: TXT
Host: google._domainkey
Value: [long DKIM value generated by Google Admin]
TTL: Automatic
4

Add DMARC monitoring

Start with monitoring. Move to quarantine/reject after legitimate email passes SPF and DKIM.

Initial DMARC record 
Type: TXT
Host: _dmarc
Value: v=DMARC1; p=none; rua=mailto:dmarc@teleotitle.com; pct=100
TTL: Automatic
Later rollout options 
# Step 2: partial quarantine after monitoring
v=DMARC1; p=quarantine; rua=mailto:dmarc@teleotitle.com; pct=25

# Step 3: final strong policy after validation
v=DMARC1; p=reject; rua=mailto:dmarc@teleotitle.com; pct=100

Final QA

Verification checklist

DNS / Email

  • MX points to smtp.google.com with priority 1
  • Only one SPF record exists
  • DKIM record was generated from Google Admin
  • Google Admin shows DKIM authentication started
  • DMARC p=none record exists
  • dmarc@teleotitle.com exists as mailbox or group

Website

  • https://teleotitle.com/sitemap.xml loads
  • https://teleotitle.com/robots.txt loads
  • GTM appears in page source
  • Google Business Profile link works
  • Google map appears on contact page
  • Schema validates with no critical errors

Marketing

  • Facebook link works
  • Instagram link works
  • LinkedIn link works
  • Newsletter form submits correctly
  • Testimonials section appears
  • Google Reviews widget loads

Tech stack

  • Order Title page exists
  • Client Portal link exists
  • Live chat widget loads
  • E-signature support section exists
  • Calendar page still works
  • Clarity or Hotjar captures sessions
