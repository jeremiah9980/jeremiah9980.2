How the test list was collected

This documents the ChatGPT-assisted seed-list process used before the public DNS/email-authentication scan.

ChatGPT-assisted collection

Prompt entered: Create me a list of realestate related domains primarily operating in williamson county

I treated this as Williamson County, Texas and built a practical seed list across brokerages/agents, title/escrow, property management, mortgage, builders, developers, apartment/community operators, and related local services with public-facing domains.

The strongest public sources were local chamber/member directories, REALTOR-related directories, and title-company office/location pages. The list was biased toward domains useful for outreach or SPF, DKIM, and DMARC scans rather than generic listing portals.
