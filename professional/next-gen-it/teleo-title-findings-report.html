Executive summary

Teleo Title has a strong public presence foundation, but weak measurement and trust controls.

The site passes the basics: it loads, HTTPS is enforced, it is mobile responsive, Georgetown is referenced in the copy, and online scheduling exists. The major gaps are not cosmetic. They affect trust, discoverability, lead capture, marketing attribution, and wire-fraud resilience.

Most urgent finding: Email/domain authentication must be remediated before scaling marketing outreach. A title company domain without strong SPF, DKIM, and DMARC controls can become a spoofing vector in real estate wire-fraud scenarios.

Risk themes

Critical findings by business impact

1

Email spoofing and wire-fraud exposure

CriticalTrust risk

The domain scan flagged missing or unverified SPF, DKIM, and DMARC controls. For a title company, this is the highest-risk gap because attackers can attempt to impersonate trusted staff or transaction communications.

2

No analytics or tag management foundation

HighMarketing blind spot

No GA4 or GTM scripts were detected, meaning the business cannot reliably measure visitors, traffic sources, conversion behavior, campaign outcomes, or retargeting audiences.

3

Local SEO trust signals are incomplete

HighLocal visibility

No Google Business Profile link, embedded map, sitemap, or structured data was detected. These are high-impact fixes for a Georgetown title company competing in local search.

4

Lead capture and social proof are weak

MediumConversion gap

The site lacks newsletter capture, testimonials, Google Reviews embed, and complete social links. This reduces credibility for agents, lenders, buyers, and sellers who are evaluating the company online.

Email security findings

Email/domain health is the highest-priority remediation area

The domain/security runbook identified Namecheap as registrar/DNS host, Vercel as web host/CDN, Next.js as platform, Google Workspace as email provider, and smtp.google.com as the MX value. SPF, DKIM, and DMARC were flagged as missing or needing setup/verification.

MX

Verify, do not assume broken

smtp.google.com is valid for Google Workspace. Confirm no conflicting records exist.

SPF

Add Google SPF

Publish v=spf1 include:_spf.google.com ~all and ensure it is the only root SPF record.

DKIM + DMARC

Generate then monitor

Generate DKIM in Google Admin, then deploy DMARC with p=none before moving to quarantine/reject.

Why it matters: Marketing increases the visibility of the domain. Before scaling outreach, the sending domain should be authenticated so messages are trusted and spoofing attempts are easier to reject.
