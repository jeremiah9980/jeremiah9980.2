Recommended Priority Order

Work from trust and deliverability first, then SEO/tracking, then conversion features.

  1. Fix DNS email authentication: SPF, DKIM, then DMARC monitoring.
  2. Verify MX: Keep Google Workspace MX if correct and remove conflicting MX records.
  3. Add GTM: Use GTM as the single tracking container.
  4. Add sitemap.xml and robots.txt: Make the site crawlable and easy to index.
  5. Add Local Business schema: Help Google understand the business entity.
  6. Add Google Business Profile link/map: Strengthen local trust signals.
  7. Add social links, newsletter, testimonials, and reviews: Improve marketing score and credibility.
  8. Add Order Title, Client Portal, Live Chat, and E-Sign support sections: Improve conversion flow.
  9. Add Clarity or Hotjar: Track how users move through the site.
  10. Re-run the audit: Validate score movement and identify remaining issues.
Important: Do not install the same tracking pixel directly and through Google Tag Manager at the same time. That can create duplicate pageviews and broken conversion data.

1. Domain & Email Security

This is the most important trust layer. Fix it before running outreach, newsletters, ads, or automated follow-up.

1

Verify Google Workspace MX

The scan shows smtp.google.com, which is the current Google Workspace MX value. Verify it in Namecheap.

Namecheap MX Record 
Type: MX
Host: @
Value: smtp.google.com
Priority: 1
TTL: Automatic
  • Go to Namecheap → Domain List → Manage → Advanced DNS.
  • Confirm the MX record above exists.
  • Remove old or conflicting MX records unless Google Admin specifically tells you to keep them.
2

Add SPF

SPF authorizes Google Workspace to send email for the domain.

SPF TXT Record 
Type: TXT
Host: @
Value: v=spf1 include:_spf.google.com ~all
TTL: Automatic
Only use one SPF record at the root domain. Multiple SPF TXT records can break SPF validation.
3

Generate and Add DKIM

Do not guess the DKIM value. Generate it from Google Admin.

  1. Open Google Admin Console.
  2. Go to Apps → Google Workspace → Gmail → Authenticate email.
  3. Select the Teleo Title domain.
  4. Generate a new DKIM record.
  5. Copy the host and TXT value into Namecheap.
  6. Return to Google Admin and click Start Authentication.
DKIM TXT Record Format 
Type: TXT
Host: google._domainkey
Value: [long DKIM value generated by Google Admin]
TTL: Automatic
4

Add DMARC Monitoring

Start with monitoring only. Move to quarantine/reject after confirming legitimate email passes SPF and DKIM.

Initial DMARC Record 
Type: TXT
Host: _dmarc
Value: v=DMARC1; p=none; rua=mailto:dmarc@teleotitle.com; pct=100
TTL: Automatic

Create this mailbox or Google Group before using the record:

dmarc@teleotitle.com
Later Rollout Options 
# Step 2: Partial quarantine after monitoring
v=DMARC1; p=quarantine; rua=mailto:dmarc@teleotitle.com; pct=25

# Step 3: Strongest final policy after validation
v=DMARC1; p=reject; rua=mailto:dmarc@teleotitle.com; pct=100

DNS / Email

  • MX points to smtp.google.com with priority 1

  • https://teleotitle.com/sitemap.xml loads
  • https://teleotitle.com/robots.txt loads

  • Google Business Profile link works
  • Google map appears on contact page

Marketing

  • Facebook link works
  • Instagram link works
  • LinkedIn link works
  • Newsletter form submits correctly
  • Testimonials section appears
  • Google Reviews widget loads

Tech Stack

  • Order Title page exists
